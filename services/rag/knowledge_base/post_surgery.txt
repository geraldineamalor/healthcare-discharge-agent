Condition: Post-Surgical Recovery

Care Instructions:
- Keep surgical wounds clean and dry.
- Take prescribed medications, including antibiotics, fully.
- Follow wound care instructions carefully.
- Avoid lifting heavy objects until approved.

Lifestyle Guidance:
- Rest adequately during recovery.
- Gradually resume activity as advised.
- Maintain proper nutrition to support healing.

Danger Signs:
- Fever or chills.
- Increasing redness, swelling, or discharge from wound.
- Severe or worsening pain.
- Difficulty breathing or chest pain.
