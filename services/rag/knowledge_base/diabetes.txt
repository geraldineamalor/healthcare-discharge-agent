Condition: Diabetes

Care Instructions:
- Take insulin or oral medications as prescribed.
- Monitor blood sugar levels regularly.
- Follow dietary recommendations provided by your healthcare provider.
- Maintain regular meal timings.
- Stay hydrated and avoid skipping meals.

Lifestyle Guidance:
- Engage in regular light to moderate physical activity.
- Avoid excessive sugar intake.
- Manage stress and sleep well.

Danger Signs:
- Very high or very low blood sugar readings.
- Dizziness, confusion, or fainting.
- Excessive thirst or frequent urination.
- Blurred vision.
- Signs of infection or slow-healing wounds.
