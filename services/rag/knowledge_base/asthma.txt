Condition: Asthma

Care Instructions:
- Use inhalers exactly as prescribed, even if symptoms improve.
- Avoid known triggers such as smoke, dust, cold air, and strong odors.
- Monitor breathing daily and note increased wheezing or shortness of breath.
- Keep rescue inhaler accessible at all times.
- Follow an asthma action plan if provided by your doctor.

Lifestyle Guidance:
- Avoid strenuous activity during flare-ups.
- Maintain good indoor air quality.
- Practice breathing exercises if advised.

Danger Signs:
- Difficulty breathing that does not improve with inhaler use.
- Persistent wheezing or coughing at night.
- Chest tightness or rapid breathing.
- Blue lips or fingernails.
- Severe shortness of breath requiring emergency care.
