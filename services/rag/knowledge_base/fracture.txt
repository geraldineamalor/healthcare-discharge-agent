Condition: Bone Fracture

Care Instructions:
- Keep the affected limb immobilized as advised.
- Do not remove casts or splints unless instructed.
- Take pain medication as prescribed.
- Elevate the injured area to reduce swelling.
- Attend all follow-up imaging or orthopedic appointments.

Lifestyle Guidance:
- Avoid weight-bearing or strenuous activity until cleared.
- Use mobility aids if recommended.
- Maintain adequate calcium and vitamin D intake.

Danger Signs:
- Increased pain, swelling, or numbness.
- Tingling or loss of movement in fingers or toes.
- Discoloration or foul odor from cast.
- Fever or signs of infection.
