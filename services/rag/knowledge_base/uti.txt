Condition: Urinary Tract Infection

Care Instructions:
- Take prescribed antibiotics exactly as directed.
- Complete the full course of antibiotics.
- Drink plenty of fluids.
- Urinate frequently and do not hold urine.

Activity Restrictions:
- Avoid strenuous activity during active infection.
- Avoid alcohol and caffeinated beverages.

Danger Signs:
- Fever above 38°C (100.4°F)
- Back or flank pain
- Blood in urine
- Persistent burning during urination
- Nausea or vomiting

Follow-Up:
- Follow up with a primary care provider if symptoms persist beyond 5–7 days.
